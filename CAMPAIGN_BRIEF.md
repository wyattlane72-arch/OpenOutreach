## product_docs (what we sell)

Lane Proven is a local media agency offering professional drone photography and
videography for businesses. Core service: aerial photo/video packages for
residential real-estate listings (exterior flyovers, lot/acreage shots,
neighborhood context shots) and for contractors (roof inspections and progress
documentation, before/after project footage for marketing, jobsite overview
shots for bids and portfolios). FAA Part 107 certified operation. Fast local
turnaround: shoot within days, edited deliverables within 48 hours. Simple
flat-rate packages starting around $150–$300 per shoot. Deliverables are
ready-to-post: MLS-sized photos, short vertical clips for social, and full
edited video.

## campaign_target (who + outcome)

Residential real-estate agents and small general/roofing/exterior contractors
in our local metro area (single-operator to ~25-employee shops). Decision
makers: the agent themself, or the owner/marketing lead at the contractor.
Outcome sought: book a 15-minute call or a first discounted shoot so they can
see deliverable quality; longer-term goal is a per-listing or monthly retainer
relationship.
